Privacy Policy

for the Android app Phaseline · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: Everything you enter in Phaseline stays on your phone. There is no account, no developer server, no ads and no analytics or advertising trackers. The developer never sees your data. The app only goes online when you look up a food online (Open Food Facts), and the Google components for barcode and text recognition send technical diagnostic data to Google. Both are described in detail below.

1. Controller and provider

Cavit Türksen
Germany
Email: phaseline@web.de

Information under Art. 13 GDPR. The fastest way to reach me is by email. Phaseline is a free app without ads and without in-app purchases. There is no data protection officer, as none is required.

2. Data on your phone

Phaseline stores data only on your device, in the app's private database and private folder. Other apps have no access, and nothing is sent to the developer. What is stored is what you enter yourself:

  • Profile: name, date of birth, height, sex, profile picture
  • Daily values: calories, macronutrients, weight, notes, meals, your own foods and recipes
  • Weekly plan, activities, workouts, check-in decisions
  • Body measurements and progress photos (location data is removed from photos when they are saved; photos do not appear in your gallery)
  • Settings, reminder times

Weight, body measurements, photos and workout data may be health data. They also stay on your phone only. Automatic Android backup to Google Drive is turned off for Phaseline. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself, without a server.

3. Backups

On request, Phaseline creates a backup file with all data, including progress photos. You decide where to save it, for example in a cloud storage service; that service's terms then apply. The developer does not receive a copy.

4. Looking up foods (Open Food Facts)

Phaseline ships with a product file (an extract of the free Open Food Facts database for Germany, Austria and Switzerland). Scanning and search look there first, offline. Only if a barcode is missing or the search finds few results does the app ask Open Food Facts online (Open Food Facts, France).

This transmits: the barcode or search term, an app identifier with the developer's contact address and, for technical reasons, your IP address. No profile or health data is transmitted. A product you select is saved on your phone.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in carrying out the search you requested). Open Food Facts privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

5. Barcode and text recognition (Google ML Kit)

Barcode scanning and reading nutrition tables from photos are done by Google ML Kit directly on the phone. Camera images, photos and recognised text do not leave the phone.

According to Google, ML Kit does send technical diagnostic and usage data to Google (Google Ireland Limited or Google LLC): device information (manufacturer, model, Android version), app information (package name, version) and per-installation identifiers that are not meant to uniquely identify a person or device. The transfer is encrypted and Google does not share the data with third parties. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working, error-free scan). Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Fitness data (Health Connect)

Only if you connect it in the app under “More” and grant the permissions, Phaseline reads from Health Connect: workouts, steps, active and total calories burned and, with your permission, distance, average heart rate and elevation gain per workout (for example from a sports watch). Phaseline does not write anything to Health Connect.

The app uses this data only to show it to you in Phaseline and to calculate your calorie needs (history, daily values, widget). It stores it only on your phone; it is part of your backup and is removed with “Delete all data”. With the “Read in background” permission, the widget updates these values about every 30 minutes. The data is not sent to the developer or third parties, not used for advertising and not sold.

The use of data from Health Connect complies with the Google Play Health Connect policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in the app or in Health Connect.

7. Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take a profile or progress photo, scan a barcode or photograph a nutrition table. No images are saved when scanning.
  • Photos: only the photo you select.
  • Notifications: for the reminders you set.
  • Health Connect: only with your consent, see section 6.
  • Internet: for sections 4 and 5.

No location, no microphone, no contacts.

8. Deletion

“Delete all data” in the app (under “More”) or uninstalling removes all data from your phone. Backups you saved elsewhere must be deleted there. As the developer holds no data about you, there is no account or server where anything would need to be deleted.

9. Google Play

If you install Phaseline via Google Play, Google processes data for this under its own terms (for example your Google account). The developer has no influence on this. Statistics that Google Play shows to developers (such as install counts or crash reports) are aggregated and do not identify you.

10. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub B.V., Netherlands, a company of GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the page and protect it against attacks; data may be transferred to the USA (GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). The page sets no cookies, uses no trackers and loads no content from other servers.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub privacy policy: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw any consent at any time. As all app data is stored on your phone only, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (view, edit, export as a backup, delete). For questions, write to phaseline@web.de.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live.

12. Children

Phaseline is not made for children and is not directed at them.

13. No medical advice

The app works with estimates. It does not replace advice from a doctor or nutrition professional.

14. Changes

If the app changes so that different data is processed, this policy will be updated. The version with the latest date at the top of this page applies.
